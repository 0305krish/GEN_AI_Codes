### **The Game We Love**

Under the morning sun, the cricket ground awakes,  
A quiet field of dreams, where every heartbeat shakes.  
The pitch lies still and silent, waiting for the day,  
While players tie their laces and prepare to play.  

The bat meets ball with thunder, a sound both sharp and sweet,  
Eleven hearts on either side, competing for the feat.  
The bowler runs with purpose, eyes fixed upon the crease,  
The batsman stands unwavering, waiting for release.  

A red ball flies like lightning, spinning through the air,  
A single wrong decision, and suddenly—despair.  
A cover drive finds the boundary, racing through the green,  
And cheers rise from the stands for the finest shot they've seen.  

The scoreboard keeps on counting, one run, then two, then four,  
Every run a little victory, every wicket something more.  
A fielder dives through dust and grass to stop the ball in flight,  
And for a brief, electric moment, everything feels right.  

The crowd begins to roar, their voices fill the sky,  
As hopes rise with every ball and fall with every try.  
A spinner turns the cricket ball, deceiving with his art,  
While the batsman reads the movement with a calm and steady heart.  

Some players chase the glory, some quietly do their part,  
For cricket is not just numbers—it is courage, skill, and heart.  
There are days of golden centuries, there are days of painful defeat,  
But every player walks away with memories bittersweet.  

A captain watches carefully, deciding when to strike,  
A bowler waits for one mistake, a batter chooses right.  
The final overs approach, the pressure starts to grow,  
One boundary can change everything, one wicket steal the show.  

The sun begins to disappear beyond the distant stand,  
Yet no one wants to leave the field, no one wants it to end.  
The final ball is delivered, the bat swings through the air,  
And thousands hold their breath to see which team will care.  

Sometimes the ball crosses the rope, and victory fills the night,  
Sometimes it finds the waiting hands, and dreams take sudden flight.  
But when the stumps are packed away and the stadium grows still,  
The love for cricket remains, as strong as ever still.  

For cricket teaches patience, to wait and then to act,  
To rise after a failure and to answer every setback.  
It teaches us that winning is not the only prize,  
For courage lives in losing when you still refuse to hide.  

From dusty village grounds to stadiums shining bright,  
The game brings people together beneath the floodlights.  
A bat, a ball, a patch of grass—that's all it takes to start,  
And somehow, in that simple game, we give away our hearts.  

So let the batsmen swing, let the bowlers chase their dreams,  
Let the fielders dive and run beneath the stadium beams.  
For as long as cricket lives, the passion will remain,  
A beautiful game of hope, of joy, of glory—and pain.
